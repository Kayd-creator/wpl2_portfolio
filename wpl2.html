WPL2.

A detailed walkthrough of the WPL2 case: context, my contribution, what I learned, and the final deliverables.

Case description

For WPL2, I worked together with a team of designers and programmers on a project for a jewelry company. We worked on the project two days a week and were responsible for creating both a website and a mobile application from scratch. The client already had a logo and an Instagram page, but the complete digital experience still had to be developed and designed by our team.

During the first phase of the project, we focused on research and analysis. We studied competitors within the jewelry industry to understand current trends and identify opportunities to create something unique for the client. Together, we discussed the strengths and weaknesses of existing brands and determined how we could differentiate the company through design, branding, and user experience.

Based on this research, we created mood boards, campaign ideas, and the first wireframes for both the website and the app. These concepts were presented to the client to gather feedback and validate our direction before continuing with the visual design and development process. After receiving feedback, we refined the concepts and moved on to designing and finalizing the interfaces.

The project also included clear professional requirements. We worked with fixed deadlines, technical requirements, and specific design expectations. To maintain good communication within the team, we held daily stand-ups where everyone shared their progress and upcoming tasks. In addition, sprint reviews and sprint retrospectives were organized regularly to evaluate the work process and improve team collaboration throughout the project.

My contribution

During this project, I focused primarily on the UI and visual design of both the website and the mobile application. I contributed to the research phase by analyzing competitors and gathering inspiration for the overall style and user experience. Based on this research, various elements such as colors, typography, and visual styles were defined. This allowed me to move forward with creating a component library.

I was responsible for designing various screens and components in Figma, ensuring that the design remained consistent across the entire platform for the client. Additionally, I worked on image generation to enhance the appearance and presentation of the jewelry, and I created different responsive versions of the website for both mobile and tablet devices.

Alongside the design work, I actively participated in team meetings, daily stand-ups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. This allowed me to clearly communicate my progress and collaborate effectively with both the designers and programmers on the team. Through this project, I further developed my skills in teamwork, UI/UX design, and working within a professional project structure with deadlines and client feedback.

What I learned

During this project, I learned how important communication and teamwork are within a professional workflow. Working together with both designers and programmers taught me how different roles contribute to the final result and how essential it is to communicate clearly during meetings, stand-ups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. I learned that good collaboration can improve both the quality of the work and the efficiency of the project.

I also improved my skills in UI/UX design by working on a real client project with specific requirements and deadlines. Creating wireframes, component libraries, and responsive layouts helped me better understand how to design consistent and user-friendly interfaces. I became more confident using Figma and learned how to organize design systems in a more professional and structured way. In addition, I gained more experience with typography, spacing, color usage, and creating visual consistency across different devices such as mobile and tablet.

Another important lesson was learning how to handle client feedback and transform it into meaningful improvements for the website and application. This experience showed me how professional projects are planned and developed step by step, starting from research and brainstorming to wireframing, designing, and finalizing the product. I also learned how important planning and time management are when working with deadlines and multiple team members. Overall, this project helped me grow both as a designer and as a team player in a professional environment.
