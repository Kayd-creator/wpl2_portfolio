RedBull
Add

A creation by myself to test how good I know Photoshop and check what I can. The design is based on a winter theme for RedBull, with a focus on the energy and refreshment that the drink provides during the cold season.

Overview

The
Challenge.

The goal of this project was to create a visually strong advertisement in Photoshop that matched the energetic identity of RedBull while also introducing a unique winter atmosphere.

I wanted to combine the cold seasonal environment with the refreshing and powerful feeling of the drink, while improving my skills in composition, lighting, blending, and photo manipulation.
